# Third-party font notices

The font files in `fonts/` are third-party font software, not Leviosa AI's work.
They are **not** covered by this package's proprietary `LICENSE`. Every bundled family
is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1** (OFL). Each family's
copyright notice is listed below, and the license text ships next to this file:

- `OFL-1.1.txt`: the full OFL 1.1 text. It applies to every family below.
- `NAVER-NANUM-LICENSE.txt`: NAVER's own publication of the OFL for the Nanum families.
  It contains NAVER's copyright line and its list of Reserved Font Names.

`catalog.json` records each family's source and license URL. `font-manifest.json`
records the exact source URL and SHA-256 of every bundled file. The fonts are
redistributed as-is and are not sold by themselves.

A copyright notice followed by "Reserved Font Name(s)" means those names are reserved
under OFL section 3. The notices below are copied from the upstream OFL headers. Where
the font file's own copyright field (name ID 0) differs, that field is quoted as well.
